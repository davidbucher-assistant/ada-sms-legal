Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect and use information when you interact with Ada by SMS. This program is conversational personal-assistant messaging for David’s coordination with people who opt in — not marketing or advertising.

How you opt in

You opt in by texting START to Ada’s SMS number (shared privately — not listed on this site) after reviewing the call-to-action and disclosures at https://davidbucher-assistant.github.io/ada-sms-legal/opt-in.html (or the same disclosures shown in the confirmation SMS). We do not buy lists or send unsolicited promotional blasts.

Information we collect

  • Your mobile phone number and the SMS content you send to or receive from Ada
  • Basic delivery metadata (timestamps, message identifiers) needed to operate the messaging service
  • Your opt-in / opt-out status for this SMS program

How we use information

We use this information only to operate Ada’s conversational SMS for David (for example replies about scheduling, errands, or other coordination you and David are discussing), to honor opt-out requests, and to run and secure the messaging service.

Message frequency varies (typically a few messages per conversation when you text Ada or when David asks Ada to reply to you). Message and data rates may apply.

Mobile information and messaging consent — no third-party marketing sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We may use service providers that are strictly necessary to deliver SMS (for example Twilio as messaging infrastructure). Those providers process data only to provide the messaging service for us and may not use your mobile number or consent for their own marketing.

Opt-out and help

Reply STOP to Ada’s number to opt out of further program messages. Reply HELP for help.

Data retention

We keep message content and related records only as long as needed to provide the assistant service, comply with law, resolve disputes, and maintain security, then delete or de-identify them.
